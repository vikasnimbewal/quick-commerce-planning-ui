How to read this sheet

Planned and firm quantities are separate source measures. Total Demand uses the calculated ledger: sales orders + net forecast after consumption + effective dependent demand. Total Supply sums the six planned and firm receipt measures. The demand detail rows retain raw inputs and therefore may not add up to the consumed total. Total Supply remains a raw receipt sum before planned/firm adjustments; dispatches are separate outbound movements and are excluded from Total Supply. Safety-stock settings show their distinct supplied values and are never summed. On-hand inventory is baseline_inventory_qty at the start of the planning cycle; later buckets remain blank rather than repeating the baseline. Ending inventory carries forward the previous signed balance plus effective supply minus net demand. Negative balances carry into subsequent periods and appear in bold red font. Dispatches are not deducted twice. Monthly ending inventory uses the stock or negative shortage from the last assigned source week, not a sum or a calendar-month-end balance. Unknown or unpublished balances remain blank (—). The display subtracts cumulative shortages from calculated physical ending stock. The engine remains unchanged. Unknown or unpublished periods leave subsequent running balances unknown.

To refresh, load the prepared and calculated CSV outputs from your latest planning run. The interface validates their keys and matching input fields before changing the sheet. Your source files are never edited. Saved runs are local browser copies, not signed-in accounts or cloud backups.
